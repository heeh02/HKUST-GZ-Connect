# Community code of conduct

HKUST(GZ) Connect welcomes respectful technical collaboration from students, staff, maintainers and
external contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence and tradeoffs rather than attacking people.
- Make scope, assumptions, conflicts of interest and AI assistance visible.
- Respect privacy, school policy, software licenses and security boundaries.
- Accept review, correct mistakes and distinguish verified results from hypotheses.
- Help newcomers reproduce issues without asking them to expose credentials or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or deliberate disruption;
- publishing private information or security details without consent;
- requesting or sharing credentials, OTPs, tokens, private captures or unauthorized vendor code;
- impersonating another contributor or hiding the accountable author of automated work;
- using project infrastructure to bypass institutional controls or harm other users.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, restrict participation, close contributions or escalate security/legal issues when needed.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where sensitive, and avoid
retaliation against good-faith reporters.
